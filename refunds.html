Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

There is no online checkout on this website. This page explains the default approach for projects discussed directly with ELREN. The written proposal or project agreement must set the final payment and cancellation terms before work starts.

Quotes and payment schedule

Fees, deposits, milestones, payment dates, taxes, and any third-party charges are confirmed in writing before a project begins. No payment is requested through this website.

Cancellation by the client

A client may request cancellation in writing by emailing hannanqaisar98@gmail.com. The parties will reconcile work completed up to the effective cancellation date, approved expenses, and any clearly disclosed non-cancellable third-party costs. The project agreement should specify any notice period or cancellation charge.

Refunds

If ELREN has been paid for a clearly identified milestone or deliverable that has not been started or delivered, the parties will review the amount attributable to that undelivered work and handle any refund in accordance with the written agreement and applicable law. Fees for work already completed, and third-party costs already incurred with the client's approval, may not be refundable where permitted by law. No blanket “no refunds” rule is intended by this page.

Defects and revisions

Any testing, acceptance period, included revisions, bug-fix window, and support obligations must be defined in the project agreement. Report an issue with a description and relevant evidence by email so it can be assessed against the agreed scope.

How to request a refund or cancellation

Email hannanqaisar98@gmail.com with your project name, the relevant invoice or milestone, and the reason for the request. Do not include payment-card details or passwords.

Refund rights may depend on mandatory consumer and contract laws. This page is a starting policy and should be reviewed by a qualified local lawyer and aligned with the actual way ELREN sells and delivers services.
